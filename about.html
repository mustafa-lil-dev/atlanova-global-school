Why Atlanova

Education that meets students wherever they are

Atlanova Global School was founded on a simple idea: a great classroom isn't defined by four walls — it's defined by great teaching, real connection, and a learning environment built with care.

Our Mission

To give students in Grades 1–8 access to a high-quality, structured education, regardless of where in the world they live.

Our Approach

We combine live, teacher-led instruction with independent learning, so students build both knowledge and the confidence to direct their own growth.

Our Community

Atlanova brings together students and families from many countries into one connected, supportive learning community.

What We Value

The principles behind every Atlanova classroom

Academic Rigor

A structured curriculum that builds real, lasting understanding.

Genuine Connection

Live classes and responsive teachers, not a self-serve course library.

Global Outlook

A classroom community that spans countries and cultures.

Family Partnership

Parents stay informed and involved through every step of the journey.
