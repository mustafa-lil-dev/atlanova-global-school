Admissions

Joining Atlanova is a guided, straightforward process

Our admissions team supports every family from first application to first day of class — wherever in the world you're applying from.

Start Your Application Talk to Admissions

How to Apply

Six steps from application to your first class

01

Submit Application

Complete our online application with student, parent/guardian, and academic information.

02

Application Review

Our admissions team reviews the application and academic background.

03

Student/Parent Meeting

A short meeting may be scheduled to learn more about your child's needs and answer any questions.

04

Admission Decision

Families receive an admission decision along with next steps.

05

Enrollment

Complete enrollment paperwork and select a tuition plan.

06

Start Learning

Students receive login access, meet their teachers, and join their first live class.

Who Can Apply

Students in Grades 1–8 from any country with a reliable internet connection are welcome to apply.

What You'll Need

Student and parent/guardian details, previous school information, and any relevant academic records.

Rolling Admissions

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis throughout the year, subject to grade-level availability.

Have questions before you apply?

Review our frequently asked questions or reach out directly to our admissions team.
