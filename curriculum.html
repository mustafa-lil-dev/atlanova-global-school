What Students Learn

A well-rounded curriculum, grade by grade

Six subject areas anchor every grade band at Atlanova. Exact scope and pacing vary by grade — your child's teacher will share detailed learning objectives at the start of each term.

English

Reading, Writing, Grammar, Literature, Communication.

Mathematics

Arithmetic, Fractions, Geometry, Algebra foundations, Problem solving.

Science

Life Science, Earth Science, Physical Science.

Social Studies

History, Geography, Civics, Culture.

Technology

Digital literacy, Computer science, Coding fundamentals.

Enrichment

Art, Health, Physical Education, and Languages.

Grade Progression

Curriculum depth increases with every grade band

Curriculum details can vary by grade and are refined each year by our academic team.

Grade 1–2

Foundational Learning

  • Phonics-based reading & early writing
  • Number sense & basic operations
  • Introductory science & social studies themes
  • Art, movement, and healthy habits
Grade 3–5

Elementary Learning

  • Fluent reading & structured writing
  • Fractions, geometry basics, multi-step problem solving
  • Life, earth & physical science units
  • Digital literacy & typing foundations
Grade 6–8

Middle School

  • Literary analysis & research writing
  • Pre-algebra & algebra foundations
  • Lab-based science & research projects
  • Coding fundamentals & foreign language
