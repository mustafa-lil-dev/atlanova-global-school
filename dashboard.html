Getting started

1. Create logins for staff/students/parents in your Supabase Authentication panel (Add user → set email + password + user metadata role and full_name).
2. Come back here to add Subjects & Classes, enroll students, and link parents to their children.
3. Teachers mark attendance and enter grades from their own login — you can also do it for them from the "Attendance & Grades" tab.
