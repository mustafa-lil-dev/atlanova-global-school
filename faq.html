Everything families ask us before applying

Atlanova currently enrolls students in Grades 1 through 8, with plans to expand to Grades 9–12 in the future.

Yes. Atlanova is a fully online school. Students attend live classes and complete independent learning from anywhere with an internet connection.

Students join scheduled online sessions led by their teacher, where they participate, ask questions, and engage with classmates in real time.

A computer or tablet with a reliable internet connection, webcam, and microphone is recommended for the best experience.

Weekly study time varies by grade level and includes both live class time and independent learning. Your admissions counselor can share grade-specific schedules.

Yes. Teachers share assignments and projects directly with students, with clear instructions and due dates. Submission currently happens through Discord and will move to our LMS once it launches.

Assessments are completed online at scheduled points throughout the term, currently coordinated through Discord, with results recorded in each student's Atlanova dashboard.

Yes. Parents get their own Atlanova login showing their child's attendance, grades, and enrolled subjects, and teachers also share updates directly through Discord and email.

Families submit an online application, which is reviewed by our admissions team. See the full process on our Admissions page.

Atlanova welcomes applications from students around the world, provided they have a reliable internet connection.

Teachers share materials and next steps so students can catch up, and small-group support is available for additional help.
