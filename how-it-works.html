The Atlanova Process

From application to graduation, here's exactly how it works

Online school can feel unfamiliar at first. Here's the full picture of what a family and student can expect at every stage.

01

Enroll

Families complete a guided online application covering student details, parent/guardian information, and academic background.

02

Meet Your Teachers

Before the term starts, students are introduced to their teachers and classmates so the first live class feels familiar.

03

Attend Live Classes

Students join scheduled online lessons where teachers introduce concepts, answer questions, and guide discussion in real time. Classes currently take place live on our Discord server.

04

Learn Independently

Between live sessions, students complete readings, practice activities, and projects at a pace that suits them.

05

Complete Assignments

Homework and projects are submitted directly through the learning management system (LMS), with clear due dates and instructions.

06

Take Assessments

Students complete quizzes and exams that help teachers and families understand how learning is progressing.

07

Track Progress

Students and parents can monitor attendance, grades, and academic progress together, all in one dashboard.

A Typical Week

Structure, with room to breathe

Every week blends scheduled live classes with independent learning blocks, so students build both knowledge and self-direction.

Live Classes

Teacher-led lessons at scheduled times throughout the week.

Small Group Support

Focused help sessions for students who need extra time on a topic.

Independent Learning

Self-paced lessons, reading, and practice between live sessions.

Teacher Feedback

Regular feedback on assignments to guide each student's next step.

The Atlanova Classroom

One dashboard for classes, assignments, and grades

Students always know what's next: today's live classes, what's due, and how they're progressing.
