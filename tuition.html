Good to Know

Tuition vs. optional fees

Included in Tuition

Live classes, LMS access, core curriculum materials, teacher support, assessments, and progress reporting.

Optional Fees

Certain enrichment activities, printed materials, or specialized programs may carry an additional fee, clearly disclosed before enrollment.
